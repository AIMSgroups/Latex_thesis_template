This ``dissertation'' provides a visual example of how a University of South Florida (USF) dissertation should be formatted. Our LaTeX thesis template follows the IEEE Computer Society (compsoc) journal style in most respects, yet it follows the USF Graduate Studies and College of Engineering electronic thesis and dissertation (ETD) guidelines where those guidelines supersede the chosen publication style. Note that the latest USF ETD guidelines always take precedence over this template or style. If conflicts arise, please inform the template maintainer so they can be promptly corrected. In general, students should thoroughly read the latest USF Graduate Studies ETD guidelines before writing. The guidelines contain a lot of important information beyond style requirements, including directions for tone of language in the thesis, suggestions for correcting common language-related errors, and procedures and deadlines for submitting the manuscript. The LaTeX template project that this document is part of was created as a way to simplify USF ETD style compliance. Beyond style information, this document will also provide instructions to properly use the LaTeX style and some general instructions for doing research and writing LaTeX documents.
